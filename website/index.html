Before we talk.

Have a different question? Start a conversation with our team.

Is StayNas only for hotels?

No. StayNas is being developed as a property operating platform for hospitality, rental apartments, commercial properties and multi-property portfolios.

Can I manage more than one property?

StayNas' commercial framework supports multi-property arrangements. The right configuration depends on your operating structure and selected plan.

Can the platform adapt to our workflows?

The platform is designed around modular capabilities. We will use a discovery conversation to understand your property model and operational requirements.

How do I get pricing?

Pricing depends on property structure, selected plan and optional capabilities. Contact the team for a commercial discussion.

Where do existing users sign in?

Existing users can access the StayNas application at app.staynas.com.
